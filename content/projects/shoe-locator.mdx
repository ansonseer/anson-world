## The problem
A Sydney footwear logistics warehouse with no location system at all. Finding goods by order
number depended entirely on one senior worker's memory. When he wasn't there, the floor slowed
to a crawl.

## The first version — built, ignored
I built V1 while employed on that floor, from first-hand observation. It worked. It was also
never deployed: shelf labels were never printed, and the owner didn't know it existed. A working
tool with no one to speak for it is just a demo.

## The delivery — via the right voice
The second run took a different path. I didn't pitch the owner; I put the tool in the hands of
the training supervisor first. He used it, then he made the case to the owner. A paid pilot was
approved and delivered: **mobile scan-to-locate, running in the phone browser (no app install),
any order found in two scans.**

## Stack
Next.js · TypeScript · Prisma · SQLite · QR scanning — deliberately boring, deliberately small.

## What it taught me
中小企业的采用决策不由技术优劣决定，由"谁替你说话"决定。
SMB adoption isn't decided by technical merit. It's decided by who speaks for you.
